I deleted things but my free space didn't change

Deleted photos and videos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, in case you change your mind. To get the space back now: open Photos, go to Collections, then Utilities › Recently Deleted. Unlock it with Face ID or your passcode, tap Select, then Delete All.

I use iCloud Photos. Where does the space come from?

Deleting a photo removes it from iCloud and from your other devices too. If "Optimize iPhone Storage" is on, your iPhone may only hold small versions of older items, so deleting those frees more iCloud space than iPhone space. Elbowroom tells you when that's the case.

Why are some videos not offered for compression?

Slow-motion, Cinematic and spatial videos are left alone so you keep their special editing. Videos that are already efficient (less than 20% smaller after compressing) are skipped. Videos stored only in iCloud need to be downloaded first; you can allow that on the Compress screen. Compressing needs free space of twice the video's size plus 1 GB.

What about System Data, Messages and WhatsApp?

No app can clean these. You can manage them yourself:

  • System Data: caches and logs managed by iOS. Restarting your iPhone and installing updates often shrinks it.
  • Messages: Settings › Apps › Messages › Keep Messages (1 Year or 30 Days), and Settings › General › iPhone Storage › Messages to review large attachments.
  • WhatsApp: in WhatsApp, Settings › Storage and Data › Manage Storage.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Elbowroom, tap the gear icon, then Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Family members with Family Sharing get the purchase automatically.

How do I ask for a refund?

In Elbowroom, open Settings and tap Request a Refund, or use reportaproblem.apple.com. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Elbowroom only sees some of my photos

You chose Limited Access. To scan the whole library, go to Settings › Apps › Elbowroom › Photos and choose Full Access.
