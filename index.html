Elbowroom

Keep what matters. Make room for the rest.

Elbowroom looks through the photos and videos on your iPhone and shows what takes up space: big videos, near-identical shots, old screenshots, screen recordings and Live Photos. You see every item before anything goes, and nothing is selected without a reason.

Coming soon to the App Store

iPhone, iOS 26 or later. Free to download, with one optional purchase. No subscription.

What it finds

Large videosSorted by size. Delete them, or shrink them to HEVC while keeping the date, place and albums.
Similar shots and burstsGrouped like a contact sheet. The sharpest one is suggested as the keeper; you can pick another.
Live PhotosKeep the photo and drop the short video, which is usually half the size.
Screenshots and screen recordingsBy month, so you can clear the old ones in a few taps.
Duplicates and blurry photosExact copies, and shots that are both soft and poorly exposed.

Honest about space

Deleted items go to the Photos app's Recently Deleted album for 30 days, so your iPhone frees the space when that album is emptied. Elbowroom says so after every action and shows you how. Sizes it can't measure exactly are marked with ≈.

System Data, Messages and other apps' storage can't be cleaned by any app. The support page explains what you can do in Settings instead.

Private by design

Your photos never leave your iPhone. Elbowroom has no account, no analytics and no servers; it works in airplane mode. Its App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected. Privacy policy.

Pay once

Screenshots, screen recordings, duplicates and deleting large videos are free, with no limits. Similar shots, blurry photos, video compression and Live Photo stills come with one purchase, shared with your family. You can try each of them free first. No subscription, nothing to cancel.
