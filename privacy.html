Elbowroom

Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026.

Elbowroom does not collect any data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Your photos and videos

Elbowroom asks for access to your photo library so it can measure items and find similar shots. All of this happens on your iPhone. Photos, videos, thumbnails and analysis results are never uploaded. The app contains no networking code of its own.

To remember what it has already analysed, Elbowroom keeps small analysis files (for example, a numeric fingerprint of each photo) in its own storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

When you compress a video or convert a Live Photo, the new file is added to your library and the original goes to Recently Deleted through Apple's Photos framework, with the system's confirmation.

Purchases

The optional purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Elbowroom only learns whether the purchase is active on your device. It never sees your payment details or Apple Account.

No tracking

There are no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries, no account and no cookies. Elbowroom does not track you across apps or websites.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, scripts or analytics, and loads no outside fonts. GitHub may log visits as described in its privacy statement.

Children

Elbowroom collects no information from anyone, including children.
